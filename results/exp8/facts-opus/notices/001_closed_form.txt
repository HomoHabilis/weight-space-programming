a clarification of rule (a). Weights you write must not come from an iterative optimization of a loss. That includes fitting a logistic regression, SVM or any other probe or classifier with an iterative solver (lbfgs, SGD, ...) and then writing its coefficients, or anything derived from them, into the model. Directions computed in closed form are fine: differences of means, LDA computed in closed form, least squares or ridge solved as a linear system, PCA/SVD, projections. If your plan uses a fitted probe as weights (for example as a gate or detector), replace it with a closed-form equivalent. Fitting a probe only to measure or diagnose (never written into weights) is still allowed.
